Meet Your Provider

Eric K. Nyaberi, PMHNP

Eric is a Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner dedicated to helping adults and adolescents achieve lasting emotional wellbeing. He offers thoughtful, evidence-based medication management and compassionate support, meeting each patient with respect, patience, and an unhurried ear.

“Healing begins when you feel truly heard. My role is to walk with you — at your pace, with care shaped around your life.”

With experience across anxiety, depression, ADHD, trauma, and mood and sleep concerns, Eric builds every plan in partnership with you — honoring your goals, your values, and your story.
